Privacy Policy

Toilet Adventure · Effective November 16, 2026 · Revised November 17, 2026
This is a translation of the Korean original. If they differ, the Korean version applies.

Toilet Adventure (the "Service") is a map service for finding public toilets and recording their condition together. The Service handles only the information it needs, and this page explains what it handles and why.

A check-in records the fact that you were near that toilet. Your exact GPS position and your route are not sent to the server; the toilet's own coordinates are. Please note, however, that a history of check-ins can reveal where and when you have been.

2. Purposes

  • Showing nearby toilets and linking to directions
  • Sharing toilet status and chat between users
  • Providing game features such as Loo Power and titles, and carrying progress across devices (when signed in)
  • Preventing spam and abuse, fixing bugs, and improving the Service

3. Retention and deletion

  • Information stored on your device is deleted when you clear the site's data in your browser.
  • Information stored on the server is kept while the Service is operated.
  • Delete it yourself: In the app (web or Android), open 🧭 Explorer's Notebook → "Delete account & data" at the bottom and tap it twice. Your posts, status reports, check-ins, added toilets, photos, coin and purchase records and profile are deleted from the server right away. If you signed in with Google, your sign-in account and account storage are deleted too. Records on your device are cleared as well.
  • If you already removed the app or can't use the button: request deletion with your nickname through ❓ Help → Send feedback on the website (leeseunggyu3.github.io/toilet-adventurer). We will delete it within 30 days after confirming.

5. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, and you can also delete it yourself in the app (see section 3). You can change your nickname and profile yourself in the app's 🧭 Explorer's Notebook. You can turn off location access in your browser settings or your phone's app settings at any time; the map still works without it.

6. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

7. Security measures

  • Posts, reports, check-ins and new toilets are saved only after an identity check with your device's secret key
  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
  • Account storage can be read and written only by its owner
  • Profiles can be changed only with your device's secret key
  • Ad rewards are granted only after the server checks the daily limit

8. Contact and changes

Please contact us through ❓ Help → Send feedback in the app. If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page with the effective date.
